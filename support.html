Account Deletion

If you want to delete your account, visit our Delete Account page.

Contact Support

For support requests, please contact us using the official support contact associated with your aein service.

When contacting support, please include enough information for us to understand the issue, but do not send passwords, authentication codes, or other sensitive credentials.

Account and Security Issues

If you believe someone has accessed your account without authorization, change your account credentials where possible and contact support as soon as possible.

Never send your password, one-time password, verification code, or other authentication credentials to support.

Privacy Requests

For privacy-related requests, please review our Privacy Policy.

If you want to request deletion of your account, visit our Delete Account page.

Before Contacting Support

If you are reporting a technical problem, it can help to include:

  • The email address associated with your account.
  • A short description of the problem.
  • The screen or feature where the problem occurred.
  • Your device type and operating system, if relevant.
  • Steps that reproduce the problem, if possible.

Please do not include passwords, OTP codes, private encryption keys, or other sensitive credentials in a support request.

Privacy & Terms

You can learn more about how aein handles information in our Privacy Policy and about use of the service in our Terms of Service.
